# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People exploring and naming feelings, using a mouse, keyboard, or touch device. The existing tool supports quick exploration and a quieter moment of reading.

## Product Purpose

Department of Emotions pairs interactive wheels with concise definitions, bodily sensations, and illustrative behaviors. The approved homepage includes Feelings, Situations, Sensations, and Behaviors. Future experiments belong on a separate draft route until the user requests promotion.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, approachable. Preserve the established warm-paper surface, pastel wheel, and unhurried reading experience.

## Anti-references

The user's prior feedback removed large headers, instruction paragraphs, redundant descriptions, carets, control rows, visible settings, and consent banners. Do not bring them back or crowd the wheel with decorative cards.

## Design Principles

- Keep the wheel the main interaction and make language easy to read.
- Keep section positions steady as selections change.
- Preserve continuity between desktop sidebars and portrait drawers.
- Give people room to explore without interpreting appearance as a diagnosis.
- Isolate drafts from the approved homepage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain roving keyboard focus, accessible names, visible focus, reduced motion, modal focus restoration, and touch selections that persist after lifting a finger. Keep reading content available when long, without clipping it. Examples vary by person and context.
